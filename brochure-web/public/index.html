Assessment & Opportunities

Seen. Assessed. Considered.

Participants may be assessed by the coaching team during the clinic. Based on assessment, eligibility, programme availability and selection criteria, outstanding players may be considered for:

  • Scholarship opportunities for selected upcoming camps and programmes
  • An invitation to the annual programme at Rafa Nadal Center in Spain
  • Exclusive discounts on selected upcoming camps in India

Opportunities are not guaranteed. Travel, accommodation and programme fees are not included unless separately confirmed. All consideration is subject to assessment and approval.

FAQ

Questions parents ask first

What is the Rafa Nadal Clinic India Tour 2027?

The inaugural season of Rafa Nadal Academy clinics across five Indian cities — a 90-minute advanced training experience for junior players, organized in India by Atletas India.

Who organizes the clinics in India?

Atletas India, a sports management company registered in Kolkata and Delhi, is responsible for local marketing, promotion and operational coordination for the tour, in partnership with Rafa Nadal Academy.

How long is each clinic?

Each participant attends one 1.5-hour (90-minute) session within a three-day city programme window. The three days are the city event window — not three full days of training per player.

Who can participate?

Junior tennis players across Beginner, Mid-Level and High Performance categories. Exact age limits — TBC before registration opens.

What happens during assessment?

Participants may be assessed by the coaching team. Detailed criteria (for example technical, tactical, movement and attitude) will be published once confirmed by the Academy.

Are scholarships or Spain invitations guaranteed?

No. Outstanding players may be considered for scholarships or an invitation to the annual programme at Rafa Nadal Center in Spain, subject to assessment, eligibility, availability and approval. Travel and fees are not included unless separately confirmed.

Are discounts available for upcoming camps in India?

Selected participants may receive exclusive discounts on upcoming camps in India, subject to published terms and availability.

What is included in the participant package?

Welcome refreshments, performance towel, shaker bottle, Academy cap (subject to approved assets), clinic-branded T-shirt, and a participation diploma (wording TBC).

How do I register for Mumbai or Bangalore?

Use the city cards above when registration portals are live. Until then, submit interest via the form on this page. Portal URLs — TBC.

How do I join the waitlist for Kolkata, Chennai or Delhi?

Select your city on the waitlist / interest form. Exact April–May 2027 dates will be announced when confirmed.

What if a clinic is full or dates change?

When a city reaches capacity, its status becomes Fully Booked and the CTA switches to Join City Waitlist. Schedule changes will be communicated to registered participants by Atletas India.

Will Rafa Nadal attend in person?

Clinics are delivered by coaches travelling from Spain (subject to confirmation). Rafa Nadal’s personal attendance is not part of this programme unless separately confirmed in writing.
