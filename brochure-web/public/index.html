The session

Why 90 minutes matters

This is not a quick look-in. It is a focused 1.5-hour promotional clinic and assessment — long enough to work, short enough to stay sharp.

See how you compete

Footwork patterns, ball striking and court positioning under coaching eyes that train to Academy standards.

Build the next step

A clear window into your tennis journey — what to keep, what to tighten, and how high-performance habits feel on court.

Leave with direction

One concentrated session designed to push intensity, not fill a timetable. Come ready to train.

International coaching

Four coaches. Travelling from Spain.

A team of four coaches from overseas / Spain is expected to deliver the clinic sessions across the India Tour. Profiles and names will be confirmed closer to each city edition — we will not invent biographies here.

Why they are coming

To bring Rafa Nadal Academy training culture onto Indian courts: technical clarity, competitive intensity, and the day-to-day habits that shape junior and high-performance players.

Their role is to coach and assess — not to promise outcomes. Personal attendance by Rafa Nadal is not implied.
