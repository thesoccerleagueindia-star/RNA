The session

Why 90 minutes matters

This is not a quick look-in. It is a focused 1.5-hour promotional clinic and assessment — long enough to work, short enough to stay sharp.

See how you compete

Footwork patterns, ball striking and court positioning under coaching eyes that train to Academy standards.

Build the next step

A clear window into your tennis journey — what to keep, what to tighten, and how high-performance habits feel on court.

Leave with direction

One concentrated session designed to push intensity, not fill a timetable. Come ready to train.

FAQ

Questions parents ask first

How do I register?

Click Register Now — a separate window opens with the registration form. Pay ₹3,850 inclusive of GST via Razorpay. Or join the waitlist on this page if you are not ready to pay.

What is the clinic fee?

₹3,850 (INR) inclusive of GST for clinic registration. Camp pricing TBA. Payment is via Razorpay.

How many slots are there per city?

Each city edition is planned for approximately 300 slots across Juniors, Mid-level and High Performance / Elite. Capacity is provisional and may be adjusted. Kolkata, Chennai and Delhi run on a first-come, first-served interest / waitlist basis until registration opens.

Who organises the tour?

ATLETAS INDIAN SPORTS AND ENTERTAINMENT PVT LTD coordinates marketing, registrations and operations in India.

Can I get a refund?

Clinic fees are non-refundable. See our Refund Policy.

Will Rafa Nadal attend?

Personal attendance is not implied. Clinics are delivered by coaches travelling from Spain (subject to confirmation).

Are scholarships or Spain invitations guaranteed?

No. Outstanding players may be considered for annual programme pathways, scholarships or a Spain invitation — all subject to assessment and approval. Registration does not guarantee any of these.

What is in the welcome pack?

Planned inclusions: clinic jersey, Rafa Nadal Academy cap, performance towel, shaker, participation diploma and welcome food pack. Final supply TBC per city.
