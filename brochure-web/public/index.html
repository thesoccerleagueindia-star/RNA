FAQ

Questions parents ask first

How do I register?

Click Register Now — a separate registration window opens with the form, inaugural discount field and payable amount (₹4,250 before discount). Or join the waitlist on this page if you are not ready to pay.

Is there an inaugural discount?

Yes. Code INAUGURAL10 gives 10% off for the first 40 confirmed registrations only. After that the code closes.

What is the clinic fee?

₹4,250 (INR) for clinic registration. Camp pricing TBA. Payments in demo mode do not charge a live card until Razorpay is configured.

Who organises the tour?

ATLETAS INDIAN SPORTS AND ENTERTAINMENT PVT LTD coordinates marketing, registrations and operations in India.

Can I get a refund?

Clinic fees are non-refundable. See our Refund Policy.

Will Rafa Nadal attend?

Personal attendance is not implied. Clinics are delivered by coaches travelling from Spain (subject to confirmation).
